# Q3 2026 Product Analytics Review

**Author:** Maria Vega, Head of Product
**Date:** September 30, 2026
**Distribution:** Executive Team
**Status:** FINAL

---

## Executive Summary

This report summarizes key product metrics for Q3 2026 (July-September), covering revenue growth, user engagement, feature adoption, and platform health. The data reflects the continued maturation of our analytics platform as we approach the 14-month mark since our Series A close.

Overall, product performance remains in positive territory, though growth rates have moderated from the exceptional levels seen immediately post-funding. The team has delivered all roadmap commitments for Q3 on schedule, including the AI-powered insights module that launched in June and is now entering its fourth month of market availability.

---

## 1. Revenue & Growth

Monthly recurring revenue closed Q3 at **$142,000**, up from $128,000 at the end of Q2. This reflects approximately **4% month-over-month growth** when averaged across the quarter.

For context, at the time of our Series A close in July 2025, MRR was growing at roughly **12% month-over-month** as we benefited from the initial marketing push and brand awareness from the funding announcement. The current 4% growth rate reflects a natural maturation curve as we scale from a smaller base.

---

## 2. Customer Satisfaction

Net Promoter Score for Q3 stands at **41**, continuing a decline from our historical high of 62 measured in Q1 of this year. This is a meaningful drop that warrants attention, though it should be noted that NPS fluctuates quarter-to-quarter and Q3 is historically our lowest-scoring quarter due to summer seasonality in user engagement patterns.

Support ticket volume increased approximately **40%** compared to Q2. The support team has attributed roughly half of this increase to the rollout of the new AI insights module, which generated a predictable spike in configuration and onboarding questions. The remaining increase appears to be general platform usage growth rather than any specific quality regression.

Customer verbatims in the NPS survey mention "steep learning curve," "too many features," and "hard to get started" — themes we have been tracking since the platform expanded beyond its original scope.

---

## 3. Feature Adoption

The **AI-Powered Insights Module**, which represented the largest engineering investment of the year at approximately **$400,000** in engineering time across two quarters, launched on June 15, 2026. After four months in market, feature adoption stands at **23%** of active users.

We should note that 23% adoption for a major new feature at the four-month mark is actually within the expected range for analytics tools, where feature discovery typically lags behind launch by 6-9 months. The team is planning an in-app onboarding flow for Q4 that should accelerate discovery.

The product team has identified that power users (top 20% by session count) are adopting the AI module at roughly 45%, while casual users show less than 10% adoption. This suggests the feature is valuable for the segment that uses the platform most intensively.

---

## 4. User Engagement

Our **DAU/MAU ratio** for Q3 is **18%**, measured as the average of daily active users divided by monthly active users. This is below the broader analytics industry benchmark of 25-35% for tools in our category. Users log in infrequently, suggesting the product is not a daily workflow tool.

---

## 5. Time to Value

**Average time-to-value** — defined as the period from account creation to first meaningful insight generated — now stands at **14 days** across new user cohorts in Q3. This is up from 7 days measured in Q4 2025, roughly eight months ago.

The team has investigated this increase and identified two contributing factors. First, the platform now has significantly more features than it did eight months ago, which increases the surface area new users must navigate. Second, as we've moved upmarket to slightly larger accounts, the decision-maker (who signs up) is often not the day-to-day user (who needs to derive value), adding organizational friction to the onboarding process.

Customer success is piloting a guided onboarding program for Q4 that targets a return to the 10-day range.

---

## 6. Platform & Mobile

**Mobile app usage** accounts for **8%** of total platform sessions in Q3, down substantially from the 22% measured at the mobile app's launch. User research with churned mobile users indicates the primary reason for leaving is that analytics workflows are perceived as a desktop-only activity.

---

## Appendix: Methodology Notes

All metrics are calculated from our internal product analytics pipeline using segment-level event data. NPS is measured via in-app survey with a response rate of approximately 12% of active users. DAU/MAU uses a 30-day rolling window for MAU and a daily snapshot for DAU. AI feature adoption is measured as the percentage of users who have used the feature at least once in the preceding 30 days.
