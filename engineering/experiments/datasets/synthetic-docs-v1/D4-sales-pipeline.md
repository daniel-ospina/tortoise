**Author:** James Chen, VP of Sales

**Distribution:** Executive Team

## Summary

This report summarizes our active sales pipeline as of the close of Q3 2026, broken down by segment, stage, and projected close dates. I want to flag upfront that the team has worked extremely hard this quarter, and while quota attainment numbers don't tell the full story, I want the exec team to have the unvarnished picture.

---

## 1. Team Performance

| Metric | Q3 2026 |
|--------|--------|
| Team quota attainment | 62% |
| Total team headcount | 4 AEs + 2 SDRs |

Quota attainment of **62%** is below where we need to be, and it is down from 71% in Q2. Average deal size in Q3 was $18,400. The sales team is composed entirely of mid-market AEs — none have prior enterprise sales experience.

---

## 2. Mid-Market Pipeline

Our core mid-market pipeline:

- **34 active deals** in pipeline
- Average ACV: **$18,000**
- Average sales cycle: approximately **3 weeks** from first contact to close
- Conversion rate from demo to close: approximately 22%
- Total pipeline value: approximately $612K in potential new ARR

The velocity of our mid-market pipeline is a genuine strength. Deals close quickly once a prospect enters evaluation — approximately 5x faster than the enterprise deals we're tracking.

---

## 3. Enterprise Pipeline (Early Stage)

We are also tracking a set of larger opportunities that fall outside our traditional mid-market ICP:

- **8 active enterprise opportunities** with organizations of 500+ employees
- Average ACV for these opportunities: **$480,000**
- Average sales cycle: approximately **3.8 months** from initial contact
- Total pipeline value: ~$3.84M in potential ARR

I want to be transparent about the status of these opportunities. These are real conversations — several were inbound from prospects who found Nexus through word of mouth or content. But our conversion capability for these deals is limited. We lost two enterprise RFPs in Q3 when the prospects requested capabilities we cannot currently offer:

- Single sign-on (SAML/OIDC) integration
- Audit logging for compliance
- Dedicated account management with defined SLAs

Our largest customers (the top 3 accounts, representing 34% of total revenue) have also requested these same features. These features are not on the current product roadmap. The demand signal is consistent across both existing accounts and pipeline prospects.

I should note: our current sales team is composed entirely of mid-market AEs. None have prior enterprise sales experience, and enterprise selling is a fundamentally different motion — longer cycles, multiple stakeholders, procurement processes, security reviews. We do not currently have the go-to-market machinery to reliably close enterprise deals. Beyond sales, the organization as a whole lacks enterprise readiness: there are no enterprise AEs, security engineers, or compliance specialists on staff. The company has 32 employees: Engineering 14, Sales 4 AEs + 2 SDRs, Customer Success 3, Marketing 3, and G&A including executive 6.

---

## 4. Near-Term Outlook

The enterprise pipeline is unlikely to produce closed revenue in Q4 given the 3.8-month average cycle and our current capability gaps. These opportunities represent future potential rather than near-term revenue.
