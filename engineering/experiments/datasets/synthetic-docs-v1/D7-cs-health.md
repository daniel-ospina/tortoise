# Customer Success Health Report — Q3 2026

**Author:** Priya Sharma, Head of Customer Success
**Date:** October 4, 2026
**Distribution:** Executive Team

---

## Overview

This quarterly report covers account health metrics, support performance, and customer sentiment trends. I'll lead with the headline: we're stretched, and it's starting to show in the numbers.

---

## 1. Team Capacity

The customer success team remains at **3 CSMs managing 180 accounts** — a ratio of **60:1**. Industry best practice for B2B SaaS at our price point is 40:1 or lower. At our current ratio, each CSM is spread across double the recommended account load.

The practical impact:

- **Average response time** has slipped to **18 hours**, up from 6 hours a year ago. For a product that customers rely on for business decisions, an 18-hour response window damages trust.
- Quarterly business reviews (QBRs) are being conducted for only top-tier accounts. Mid-tier accounts receive automated check-in emails, not personal outreach.
- Two CSMs have flagged burnout concerns in their 1:1s. Neither has given notice, but I want the exec team aware that we're running at unsustainable capacity.

---

## 2. NPS & Satisfaction Trends

**NPS dropped from 62 to 41** over the past 6 months. I want to provide color beyond the headline number.

The NPS survey verbatims cluster around several themes:

**Negative themes (detractors):**
- "Product has become too complex" (mentioned by 18 respondents)
- "Hard to get started / steep learning curve" (14 respondents)
- "Support takes too long to respond" (11 respondents)
- "Mobile app is basically useless now" (9 respondents)
- "Features I don't need keep getting added, features I need don't exist" (7 respondents)

**Positive themes (promoters):**
- "Core analytics are solid and reliable" (22 respondents)
- "Great value for the price" (15 respondents)
- "CSM [name] has been incredibly helpful" (12 respondents)

The pattern is consistent: our power users love the core product but are frustrated by the complexity creep. Newer users struggle with onboarding and time-to-value.

---

## 3. Time-to-Value Trends

We track time-to-first-insight as a leading indicator of account health. The trend is moving in the wrong direction:

| Quarter | Avg Time-to-Value | Change |
|---------|-------------------|--------|
| Q4 2025 | 7 days | — |
| Q1 2026 | 9 days | +2 |
| Q2 2026 | 11 days | +2 |
| Q3 2026 | 14 days | +3 |

The doubling from 7 to 14 days over approximately three quarters is concerning. Accounts with longer TTV are 3x more likely to churn within the first year.

I've proposed a guided onboarding program to the product team that would pair new accounts with a CSM for their first two weeks. This program requires additional CS headcount, which has not been approved.

---

## 4. Support Ticket Analysis

Support ticket volume increased **40%** quarter-over-quarter. The breakdown: approximately 45% product usage questions, 25% bug reports, 20% feature requests (including for enterprise features: SSO, audit logs, and custom permissions), and 10% billing/account questions. The product usage category is where we're seeing the most growth, which aligns with the increasing complexity of the platform.

---

## 5. Churn Risk

We currently have 12 accounts flagged as high churn risk based on a combination of: NPS score, support ticket volume, and declining login frequency. Combined, these accounts represent approximately $38,000 in MRR, or 27% of total revenue. Several of these high-risk accounts are among our largest customers — specifically, the top 3 accounts representing 34% of total revenue — and they have specifically requested enterprise features (SSO, audit trails, custom SLAs) that are not on our current roadmap.

Two CSMs have flagged burnout concerns in their 1:1s. Quarterly business reviews are being conducted for only top-tier accounts; mid-tier accounts receive automated check-ins.

---

*Survey methodology: NPS measured via in-app popup to active users (12% response rate). Time-to-value measured from account creation to first dashboard insight generated. Questions to priya@nexusanalytics.com.*
