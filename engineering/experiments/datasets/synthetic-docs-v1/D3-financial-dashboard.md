## Dashboard Summary

This report provides a snapshot of Nexus Analytics' financial position at the close of Q3 2026. All figures are unaudited and drawn from our internal financial systems as of September 30.

---

## 1. Cash Position & Runway

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Cash on hand | $4,200,000 |
| Q3 average monthly burn | $300,000 |
| Estimated runway | ~14 months |

Cash reserves remain healthy in absolute terms, though the burn rate has increased meaningfully since our Series A close. At the time of Series A (July 2025), monthly burn was approximately $220,000. The increase to $300,000 reflects our headcount growth from 23 to 32 employees over the intervening 14 months — a 40% increase — as we invested in engineering, product, and go-to-market functions.

The 14-month runway projection assumes burn remains constant at current levels and that revenue growth continues at its current trajectory, partially offsetting operating expenses. This projection does not account for any additional fundraising or major strategic shifts that could alter either the revenue or expense side of the equation.

---

## 2. Unit Economics

| Metric | Current | Series A (Jul 2025) | Benchmark |
|--------|---------|---------------------|-----------|
| Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) | $8,400 | $3,200 | Varies by segment |
| LTV:CAC Ratio | 2.8:1 | 5.1:1 | ≥3.0:1 |

**The LTV:CAC ratio has declined from 5.1:1 to 2.8:1.** This is now below the industry benchmark of 3.0:1 for healthy unit economics.

The primary driver is CAC inflation. Our cost to acquire a new customer has nearly tripled (from $3,200 to $8,400) as our primary paid acquisition channels have shown clear signs of saturation with diminishing returns.

Lifetime value is approximately $23,500 per customer, up from $16,300 at Series A — driven by expansion revenue from existing accounts. However, the LTV growth rate (44%) has not kept pace with the CAC growth rate (163%), compressing the ratio below the sustainability threshold.

---

## 3. Revenue Trajectory

Monthly recurring revenue closed Q3 at $142,000, representing 4% month-over-month growth. At the time of Series A (July 2025), growth was at 12% month-over-month — the deceleration is consistent with SaaS maturation. At the current 4% growth rate, MRR would reach approximately $180,000 in six months, $225,000 in twelve months, and $340,000 in twenty-four months.

The board has previously discussed a target of $200,000 MRR as a milestone for the next fundraising conversation. At current growth rates, this milestone would be reached in approximately 9 months.

Operating costs at current 32-person headcount total approximately $275K-$300K per month. The gap between revenue and costs is funded by cash reserves, which are depleting by roughly $160K per month. Breakeven at approximately $300K MRR would occur around month 20-22 if nothing changes.

---

## 4. Cost Structure

Our current cost structure reflects a company organized around mid-market PLG with a 32-person team. Team breakdown: Engineering 14, Sales 4 AEs plus 2 SDRs, Customer Success 3, Marketing 3, G&A including executive 6.

We do not currently employ any enterprise account executives, security engineers, or compliance specialists. Any strategic shift would require new hires in these categories.
