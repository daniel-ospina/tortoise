now at the **token** cap, not the byte cap (below).

### Reader-pin durability (why `docs/runbook/ask_shape_run.py` exists)

`pinned_reader_env()` pops `OPENROUTER_API_KEY`, but `tortoise/mcp_server._load_dotenv()` runs at
**import** time and only fills keys **absent** from `os.environ`; the instrument imports `mcp_server`
later (per question, in `_shipping_handlers`), so the key was re-injected from the repo `.env` and
the **#476 fallback was re-armed**. On the pre-fix attempt `1de5cff2` hopped to `openrouter` and the
instrument correctly reported `PROVIDER VOID`. The launcher imports `mcp_server` once *before* the
pin, making the pop durable; it changes nothing about the measurement. With it,
`providers_observed == ['deepseek-direct']` on all 21 questions of both passes. Filed as **#4582**.
