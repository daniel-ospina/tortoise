# IP Assignment & License Agreement

**Parties:** Daniel Ospina ("Daniel") and Connor [Lastname] ("Connor")

**Background:** Connor and Daniel have collaboratively developed intellectual property related to epistemic belief propagation in knowledge graphs, including typed logical operator edges (IMPL/NAND) and belief propagation algorithms (the "Shared IP"). Connor wishes to assign his rights in the Shared IP to Daniel (or his designated entity, the "Company") in exchange for equity and a license-back, with the assignment being conditional on the Company achieving meaningful commercial traction.

---

## 1. IP Assignment

**1.1 Assignment.** Connor hereby assigns, transfers, and conveys to Daniel (or his designated entity, the "Company") all of his right, title, and interest in and to the Shared IP, including all patent rights, copyrights, trade secrets, and any other intellectual property rights therein.

**1.2 Shared IP Definition.** "Shared IP" means all inventions, discoveries, designs, algorithms, and know-how developed by Connor individually or jointly with Daniel prior to the Effective Date relating to: (a) knowledge graphs where edges represent typed logical operators (IMPL, NAND, SUPERSEDES, MITIGATES); (b) methods for propagating confidence or belief scores through such graphs; (c) the specific implementation known as "Tortoise" at github.com/daniel-ospina/premise-labs.

**1.3 Further Assurances.** Connor agrees to execute all documents reasonably necessary to perfect this assignment, including patent assignments, inventor declarations, and powers of attorney.

---

## 2. Condition & Reversion

**2.1 Traction Milestone.** The assignment in Section 1 shall become permanent and irrevocable upon the Company achieving ANY of the following within 24 months of the Effective Date (the "Milestone"):

(a) Raising at least USD $300,000 in aggregate financing (equity, SAFE, convertible note, or grant); or

(b) Achieving at least USD $10,000 in monthly recurring revenue (MRR), sustained for at least two consecutive months, from a product or service primarily based on the Shared IP.

Daniel shall provide Connor with written notice and reasonable evidence within 30 days of achieving the Milestone.

**2.2 Reversion.** If the Milestone is NOT achieved within 24 months, the assignment in Section 1 shall be void ab initio, and all rights in the Shared IP shall automatically revert to Connor, with each Party retaining the rights they held prior to this Agreement. The license granted in Section 4 shall survive such reversion and become perpetual.

**2.3 Cessation.** If the Company ceases active operations for 90 consecutive days before the Milestone is achieved, the assignment shall similarly revert.

---

## 3. Equity

**3.1 Grant.** Within 60 days of the Milestone being achieved, the Company shall grant Connor equity representing two percent (2.0%) of the fully-diluted capitalization of the Company.

**3.2 Terms.** The equity shall be restricted stock (or stock options, at Connor's election), subject to: (a) 4-year vesting with a 1-year cliff measured from the Milestone date; (b) standard drag-along, tag-along, and right of first refusal provisions; (c) 83(b) election to be filed by Connor within 30 days of receipt if restricted stock is elected.

**3.3 Valuation.** The equity shall be valued at the fair market value of the Company as of the Milestone date, as determined by the Company's board or a 409A valuation.

---

## 4. License-Back to Connor

**4.1 Grant.** Daniel (or the Company) hereby grants Connor a non-exclusive, worldwide, perpetual, irrevocable, royalty-free, non-transferable, non-sublicensable license under the Shared IP and any patents issuing therefrom to make, have made, use, offer to sell, sell, and import products and services in ALL fields of use EXCEPT the Excluded Field.

**4.2 Excluded Field.** "Excluded Field" means: offering, selling, or providing a product or service whose PRIMARY function is agent memory, knowledge graph, or epistemic reasoning infrastructure made available to third parties as a standalone product or service. For clarity: (a) embedding the Shared IP in a product whose primary function is NOT agent memory infrastructure is expressly permitted; (b) using the Shared IP for consulting, professional services, and internal operations is expressly permitted.

**4.3 Transfer.** This license is personal to Connor and may not be transferred or assigned except in connection with the sale of all or substantially all of Connor's business (whether by merger, stock sale, or asset sale), provided the acquirer is not a direct competitor of the Company in the Excluded Field.

**4.4 Survival.** This license shall survive any termination, expiration, or reversion under this Agreement.

---

## 5. Representations

Each Party represents that: (a) they have the right to enter into this Agreement; (b) they are not under any conflicting obligation to any third party; (c) they have disclosed to the other Party all contributions made to the Shared IP.

---

## 6. Patent Cooperation

Either Party may file patent applications covering the Shared IP. The filing Party shall notify the other within 30 days and provide a copy. Each Party agrees to execute all documents necessary to perfect such filings. Patent costs shall be borne by the filing Party; if the Milestone is achieved, the Company shall reimburse Connor for reasonable patent costs previously incurred, up to USD $5,000.

---

**7.2** Disputes: binding arbitration in Wilmington, DE under AAA rules.  
**7.3** This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding between the Parties.  
**7.4** Amendment only by written instrument signed by both Parties.
