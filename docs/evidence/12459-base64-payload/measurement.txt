  b85/z85 are 25 % overhead against base64's 33.3 % — cheaper by 128 B on this field. base64 is chosen for ubiquity and hand-inspectability (most languages' stdlibs: Python, Java, Go, C#, Node -- not Rust's or C's, the same reason b85's support is thin -- and `echo | base64 -d`), not for being the smallest.
