Automatically ingest agent sessions from any harness, GitHub issues, documents, chat messages, systems of record, BI, or any other data you want to connect.

Our ontology engine builds a logical model of how your business, product, and code work, turning loose facts into knowledge. With deduplication and superseding. This logical model enables your agents to not just retrieve facts but understand your logic, eliminating drift and regressions.

Improve decision-making with super intelligence, grounded in your company’s knowledge and fully auditable. Thanks to an algorithm inspired by interconnected prediction markets, Tortoise can compute hundreds or even millions of data points.
