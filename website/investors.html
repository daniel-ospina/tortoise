Tortoise - the cognitive layer for agentic organizations

The Problem

Agents can make decisions. They struggle to keep them consistent as things change.

More agents, faster execution → More assumptions and dependencies → coordination failures at machine speed:

  • Local fixes that create problems elsewhere
  • Repeated work and outdated decisions
  • Self-propagating Bureaucracy

WHY NOW

  • Large teams → Small teams, enterprise-scale complexityLean teams operate functions previously requiring entire departments.
  • Periodic coordination → Continuous adaptationWork moves faster than human coordination cycles.
  • Human knowledge → Machine-computable understandingCollective reasoning and organizational learning, not just information retrieval.

Tortoise provides an agent-native cognitive layer: reasoning, memory, learning.

Connect documents, systems of record, BI, agent sessions, or conversation streams. Tortoise builds a logical model of your organization (plug-and-play epistemics).

Agents can compute over broad organizational context, unlocking autonomy, distributed reasoning, and learning. Your organization operates coherently at machine speed.
