Tortoise - Enabling agents to reason, learn, and coordinate as an organization.

The Problem

Agents make decisions faster than they can keep them consistent.

More agents, faster execution → More assumptions and dependencies → coordination failures at machine speed:

  • Local fixes that create problems elsewhere
  • Repeated work and incoherent decisions
  • Self-propagating Bureaucracy

Capability Gaps

  • Harnesses coordinate execution, but reasoning is agent-local.
  • Agent Memory Systems retrieve information, but don’t model its logical implications.
  • Enterprise Intelligence Platforms model business operations and decisions, but assume substantial integration and ontology modeling.
  • Chat platforms and forums enable communication but also create noise and sidetrack agents.

Agentic organizations lack a system to understand and reason across the broader organizational context - a shared cognitive layer. But they can’t afford months of modeling ontologies.

Why Now

  • Lean teams → Large agent workforces
  • Human-paced bureaucracy → Machine-speed coordination
  • Human knowledge → Machine-computable understanding

Tortoise gives agent teams shared memory, reasoning, and learning.

  1. Onboard in 5 minutes: Agents use Tortoise to analyze complex decisions without losing track of variables or assumptions. The reasoning gets saved as memory.
  2. As agents work and new sources (documents, systems of record, BI, etc) are connected, Tortoise builds a logical model of the organization. Agents use this model to evaluate alternatives, calculate implications, and understand how their decisions affect other work.
  3. When new evidence arrives, the model recalculates dependent beliefs, allowing agents to build on each other’s learning and adapt their decisions.

The result: autonomous agents that can coordinate, learn, and adapt together at machine speed.

  • ↑Token efficiency
  • ↑Decision quality
  • ↑Agent Autonomy
  • ↓Coordination overhead
  • ↓Drift & Duplication
  • ↓Human supervision

How it works

LLMs identify the premises; Tortoise connects and computes their implications.

  • Eliminate biases: frequency and recency optimize search speed but don’t determine truth.
  • Argument graphs: logical chains (A-contradicts-B-supports-C) map reasoning; system performance improves as models become cheaper.
  • Traceable synthesis, not compression: Inspired by interconnected prediction markets, our algorithm resolves confidence across dependent claims. Evidence links to conclusions.
  • Continuous learning: New evidence recalculates dependent conclusions, enabling agents to adapt and trigger automations.
