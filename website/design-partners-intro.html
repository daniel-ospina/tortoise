Design Partner Introduction

Tortoise — context and reasoning infrastructure for agents.

The problem

Agents are increasingly capable, but fundamentally struggle with complex work that unfolds over time. They forget previous decisions, lose important variables as context grows, sub-optimise, and can be disproportionately influenced by the latest information.

Regular memory systems are filing cabinets, providing retrieval of facts but unable to provide an understanding of how the pieces of a company, product, or technical system work together.

How it works

Tortoise provides “computational” memory: it connects state (what is), events (when), evidence (sources), and beliefs (why) through logical chains (support, contradict, etc) and quantifies the relationships: as new evidence arrives, the system learns by propagating confidence. This enables agents to align with the bigger picture, reason around tradeoffs, and understand downstream consequences.
